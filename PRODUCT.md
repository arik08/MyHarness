# Product

## Register

product

## Users

MyHarness is used by company office workers who want to try, understand, and apply AI agents to real work through a browser. Users need visible execution steps, workspace-scoped history, reusable skills, and direct previews of generated artifacts without learning a developer-oriented terminal workflow.

## Product Purpose

MyHarness makes agent workflows approachable and operationally transparent inside the company. It should help a user move from a work request to a reviewable result while making the agent's actions, files, current state, and administrative controls easy to understand. Success means users can work confidently without needing to understand the underlying runtime.

## Brand Personality

Clear, dependable, and restrained. The interface should feel like a focused professional tool: calm during long-running work, explicit about system state, and compact enough for frequent daily use.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic marketing-dashboard styling, oversized cards, excessive rounding, thick padding, decorative gradients, and motion that competes with the task. Avoid browser-native title tooltips, hidden progress, ambiguous controls, and visual treatments that make settings or operational state look less consequential than they are.

## Design Principles

1. Show the work and current state so users are never left guessing during long-running tasks.
2. Prefer familiar, compact controls that disappear into the workflow.
3. Keep user, workspace, and administrator scope explicit wherever settings or actions differ.
4. Make generated artifacts directly reviewable and keep their relationship to the active conversation clear.
5. Preserve operational trust through actionable errors, reversible choices, and consistent behavior across reconnects.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, keyboard-operable interactions, visible focus states, readable Korean and English labels, sufficient contrast across supported themes, and text or shape cues in addition to color. Respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid interactions that depend on hover alone.
